About Deluge Integration

The Deluge integration allows you to connect to your Deluge WebUI and manage your torrents directly from this application.

Features:

  • Connect to multiple Deluge servers
  • View torrent status and progress
  • Add new torrents
  • Pause, resume, and remove torrents

For more information, please refer to the Deluge documentation.
